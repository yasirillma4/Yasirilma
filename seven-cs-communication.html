The Seven C's of
Effective Communication

Introduction

Why Communication Matters

Effective communication is not just about speaking or writing. It is about ensuring the receiver understands the message exactly as the sender intended.

Clarity of Purpose

Reducing ambiguity ensures the core message lands without confusion or misinterpretation.

Efficiency

Concise and coherent communication saves time for both the sender and the receiver.

Relationship Building

Courtesy and correctness foster trust and professionalism in academic and business settings.

01 / The Seven C's

Clarity

Clarity means the message is easy to understand. It focuses on a single purpose and uses simple, direct language to avoid confusion.

Real World: Road signs saying "STOP"
Unclear
Vague Instruction

"Submit your assignment soon."

Clear
Specific Deadline

"Submit your assignment by Friday, 5 PM."

02 / The Seven C's

Conciseness

Saying more with less. Conciseness eliminates unnecessary words and repetition while retaining the full meaning.

Real World: Nike's "Just Do It"
Wordy
Redundant

"Due to the fact that the exam is near, it is necessary that students should prepare."

Concise
Direct

"Students should prepare because the exam is near."

03 / The Seven C's

Concreteness

Being specific and definite. Concrete communication uses facts, figures, and vivid words rather than vague generalizations.

Real World: "Delivery in 25 mins"
Vague
Generalization

"Many students passed the exam."

Concrete
Data-Driven

"80% of the students passed the exam."

04 / The Seven C's

Correctness

Ensuring accuracy in grammar, spelling, facts, and figures. Correctness builds credibility and prevents costly errors.

Real World: Exam Hall Tickets
Incorrect
Grammar Error

"The teacher say that tomorrow is holiday."

Correct
Professional

"The teacher says that tomorrow is a holiday."

05 / The Seven C's

Coherence

Logical flow. All ideas should be connected and arranged in a sequence that makes sense to the receiver.

Real World: Storytelling Arcs
Disconnected
Fragmented

"I was sick. The class was online. My phone was not working."

Connected
Logical Flow

"I could not attend the online class because I was sick and my phone failed."

06 / The Seven C's

Completeness

Providing all necessary information. A complete message answers Who, What, Where, When, Why, and How.

Real World: Event Invitations
Incomplete
Missing Details

"We have a meeting tomorrow."

Complete
All Details

"Meeting tomorrow at 10 AM in Room 5."

07 / The Seven C's

Courtesy

Politeness and respect. Courteous communication considers the receiver's feelings and builds positive relationships.

Real World: Customer Support
Rude
Demanding

"Give me the notebook."

Polite
Respectful

"Could you please pass me the notebook?"
